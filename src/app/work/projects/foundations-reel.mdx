# Projects

## Digital Preservation Experiment

Blender project involving photogrammetry, 3D modeling,  360° environment capture with a curiosity for documenting historical spaces. Made up of three parts. 

1. Glass bottle modeled in Blender with precise measurements and gaussian splat data for accurate proportions. Details and irregularities sculpted in ZBrush were baked into a displacement map for a convincing 3D profile without the added geometry. Finally, much of the time went into lookdev for the glass material. I tried to get as close to the real thing as possible, finetuning the color, opacity and surface details like scratches and smudges.
2. Visited a local historic site and captured it with a 360° camera, then built an HDRI in Photoshop for immersive, accurate lighting in the Blender scene.
3. Scanned elements of the site with traditional photogrammetry, giving mesh, normals and textures that I then manually adjusted so it blends seamlessly with the rest of the scene

It came together well, every part of the scene feels cohesive even though it’s built from a mix of photography, 3D scanning, and 3D modeling. I learned a lot across Blender and ZBrush and I still have a strong interest in photogrammetry and custom HDRIs for the preservation of historical spaces and artifacts.

## Sphinx Sculpt

This ZBrush creature sculpt demonstrates my understanding of both human and animal anatomy from my traditional drawing and sculpting classes. 

The full process also reflects various ZBrush techniques and tools I learned, including creating  custom IMM and texture brushes, Nanomesh to generate the feathers, and ZSpheres for rigging and posing the final piece, entirely within ZBrush.

A comedic fantasy pet-sim-meets strategy game, developed with GB Studio and its GameBoy style constraints. You play as the Dark Lord caring for a little grub that you raise into an inspiring Troll General, feeding and training your troll as it grows in order to meet the class-specific score thresholds you need to secure a victory. 

This was a small team project where we all collaborated on the gameplay design and prototyping, but my role as the programmer overlapped a lot with the art side of things. GB Studio’s limitations mean a lot of technical problem solving comes down to creative use of pixel art assets, particularly with the UI, which was also my work.
